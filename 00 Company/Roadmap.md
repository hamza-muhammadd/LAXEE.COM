# LAXEE — Master Roadmap

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Purpose:** The sequence in which LAXEE becomes a validated, operating business.

This is the master sequence. Detailed work belongs in GitHub Issues and Projects.

---

# Phase 0 — Clean & Establish

**Objective:** Create one coherent source of truth.

### Work

- Clean legacy repository
- Remove empty placeholders
- Consolidate duplicate research
- Establish folder ownership
- Establish document authority rules
- Establish decision log
- Establish operating manual

### Exit condition

A new LAXEE document can be placed in exactly one obvious location, and obsolete material is clearly archived or deleted.

**Current phase:** FINAL CLEANUP / COMPLETION

---

# Phase 1 — Brand & Company Foundation

**Objective:** Make the strategic identity of LAXEE coherent.

### Work

- Vision
- Mission
- Values
- Brand foundation
- Positioning
- Differentiation
- Brand voice
- Visual direction
- Customer experience principles

### Exit condition

Core company and brand decisions are explicit, consistent, and usable for product and execution decisions.

---

# Phase 2 — Research & Validation

**Objective:** Replace important assumptions with evidence.

### Work

- Market validation
- Customer validation
- Competitor benchmark
- Category analysis
- Pricing validation
- Product demand validation
- Supplier feasibility
- Packaging feasibility

### Exit condition

Important assumptions are either validated, disproven, or explicitly retained as hypotheses with a test plan.

---

# Phase 3 — Nikah Signature Product

**Objective:** Develop and validate the first LAXEE product proposition.

### Work

1. Candidate selection
2. Supplier sourcing
3. Samples
4. Quality checks
5. Product composition
6. Costing
7. Pricing hypothesis
8. Packaging prototype
9. Personalization system
10. Customer/concept validation
11. Product specification
12. Final approval

### Exit condition

Nikah Signature is a defined, sourced, costed, tested, producible product with an approved specification.

**Current product priority:** NIKAH SIGNATURE

---

# Phase 4 — Packaging & Experience

**Objective:** Turn the product into a complete LAXEE gifting experience.

### Work

- Structural packaging
- Materials
- Visual application
- Inserts
- Cards
- Personalization
- Opening sequence
- Protection
- Quality control
- Unboxing experience

### Exit condition

The complete physical experience is production-ready and consistent with LAXEE.

---

# Phase 5 — Commercial Readiness

**Objective:** Make the complete customer journey sellable.

### Work

- Product photography
- Product copy
- Website
- Product pages
- Pricing
- Checkout
- Delivery
- Customer service
- Policies
- Supplier network
- Fulfillment SOPs
- Inventory controls
- Quality controls

### Exit condition

A real customer can discover, understand, purchase, personalize, receive, and experience LAXEE reliably.

---

# Phase 6 — Controlled Launch

**Objective:** Launch small enough to learn and strong enough to protect the brand.

### Work

- Launch readiness check
- Initial inventory / production capacity
- Website release
- Marketing assets
- Customer support readiness
- Order fulfillment
- Feedback capture
- Issue tracking
- Measurement

### Exit condition

Real customers have completed the journey and LAXEE has reliable evidence about product, experience, economics, and operations.

---

# Phase 7 — Improve Before Expanding

**Objective:** Fix the first system before adding complexity.

### Work

- Customer feedback analysis
- Product improvements
- Packaging improvements
- Operational improvements
- Supplier improvements
- Margin improvement
- Conversion improvement
- Repeat purchase / referral learning

### Exit condition

The first operating system is stable enough to justify additional complexity.

---

# Phase 8 — Premium & Prestige

**Objective:** Expand the validated Nikah architecture.

### Order

`Signature → Premium → Prestige`

Premium should only be developed after Signature has enough evidence and operational stability.

Prestige remains intentionally limited and bespoke.

---

# Phase 9 — New Collections & Occasions

**Objective:** Expand only where evidence supports it.

Potential future areas:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Corporate gifting
- Islamic occasions
- Other culturally meaningful gifting occasions
- Future product categories

No new collection is approved merely because it is an interesting idea.

---

# Phase 10 — Scale the Operating System

**Objective:** Turn LAXEE into a repeatable company rather than a founder-dependent project.

### Work

- SOP maturity
- Supplier redundancy
- Team ownership
- Automation
- Analytics
- Quality management
- Financial controls
- Customer retention
- Product portfolio management
- Governance

### Principle

> **Scale what works. Do not scale confusion.**

---

# Current Priority Stack

Until Phase 3 is sufficiently complete, priorities should generally follow:

```text
1. Repository / operating-system cleanup
2. Brand foundation
3. Research validation
4. Nikah Signature
5. Packaging
6. Supplier feasibility
7. Customer experience
8. Commercial readiness
9. Controlled launch
```

Do not let future collections, elaborate marketing, or unnecessary website complexity distract from validating the first product system.

---

# Phase Gate Rule

Do not advance simply because a document is complete.

Advance when the **exit condition** is satisfied.

A phase may remain open while another phase has small preparatory work happening in parallel, but the main strategic focus must remain narrow.
