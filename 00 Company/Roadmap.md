# LAXEE — Roadmap

This roadmap describes the major sequence of work. It is intentionally high-level; detailed tasks belong in GitHub Issues.

## Phase 01 — Foundation

**Objective:** Establish a coherent company and brand system.

- Company vision and mission
- Brand foundation
- Brand positioning
- Brand identity direction
- Brand voice
- Operating system

**Exit condition:** LAXEE has one coherent strategic foundation and no major contradiction between core documents.

## Phase 02 — Validation

**Objective:** Replace assumptions with evidence.

- Market research
- Customer research
- Competitor research
- Category analysis
- Pricing research
- Product demand validation

**Exit condition:** Major positioning, customer, product, and pricing assumptions have evidence or are explicitly marked as hypotheses.

## Phase 03 — Product Development

**Objective:** Turn the initial product architecture into production-ready products.

- Nikah Signature
- Nikah Premium
- Nikah Prestige
- Product specifications
- Prototypes
- Costing
- Quality standards
- Personalization

**Exit condition:** Initial products can be sourced, produced, priced, packaged, and fulfilled consistently.

## Phase 04 — Packaging System

**Objective:** Create a repeatable physical LAXEE experience.

- Structural concepts
- Material selection
- Visual application
- Inserts
- Cards
- Opening sequence
- Protection
- Supplier validation
- Quality control

**Exit condition:** Packaging is production-ready and consistent with the brand system.

## Phase 05 — Commercial System

**Objective:** Make LAXEE ready to sell.

- Website
- Product content
- Photography
- Pricing
- Checkout
- Delivery
- Customer service
- Policies
- Supplier network
- Fulfillment SOPs

**Exit condition:** A customer can complete the entire journey reliably.

## Phase 06 — Launch

**Objective:** Launch the first validated LAXEE offering.

- Launch plan
- Inventory
- Quality checks
- Marketing assets
- Website release
- Customer support readiness
- Post-launch measurement

**Exit condition:** First customers receive the intended LAXEE experience and feedback is captured.

## Phase 07 — Improvement & Expansion

**Objective:** Improve the system before expanding complexity.

- Analyze customer feedback
- Improve products
- Improve operations
- Improve packaging
- Expand collections
- Expand occasions
- Develop corporate gifting
- Explore new markets

**Principle:** Scale what works; do not scale confusion.

---

## Roadmap Rule

The roadmap is not a task list. It defines the sequence of business maturity.

Tasks should be created as GitHub Issues and linked conceptually to the relevant phase.
