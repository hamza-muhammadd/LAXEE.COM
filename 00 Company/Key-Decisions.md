# LAXEE — Key Decisions

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Purpose:** Single record of major decisions that change LAXEE's direction, architecture, positioning, product system, or operating model.

## How to Record a Decision

For every important decision, record:

- **Date**
- **Decision**
- **Status** — Proposed / Active / Replaced
- **Reason**
- **Evidence / source documents**
- **Alternatives considered** when material
- **Impact**
- **Owner**
- **Review condition** if applicable

A decision does not need to be permanent. If it changes, create a new decision and mark the old one **Replaced**.

# Active Decisions

## 2026-08 — LAXEE as a Premium Gifting House

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** LAXEE will be positioned as a premium gifting house rather than a conventional gift shop.

**Reason:** The intended competitive advantage is curation, presentation, personalization, service, and experience rather than product volume alone.

**Impact:** Product, packaging, website, customer experience, growth, and future retail decisions should reinforce the gifting-house model.

---

## 2026-08 — Quiet Opulence

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** “Quiet Opulence” is the current LAXEE brand motto and guiding aesthetic principle.

**Reason:** The premium position depends on restraint, quality, intentionality, and emotional sophistication rather than visual excess.

**Impact:** Brand identity, packaging, photography, digital design, communication, and customer experience should be evaluated against this principle.

---

## 2026-08 — Nikah as Initial Product Focus

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** Nikah is the first major LAXEE product collection to develop and validate.

**Reason:** Existing research provides a stronger starting point for a purpose-built Nikah gifting proposition than for unvalidated future collections.

**Impact:** Current product-development resources should prioritize Nikah rather than expanding into multiple occasions simultaneously.

---

## 2026-08 — Nikah Tier Architecture

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** The Nikah collection uses `Signature → Premium → Prestige`.

**Reason:** The architecture creates a clear progression of experience without relying simply on adding more products.

**Impact:** The old `Elite` tier name is retired. Signature is the current development priority; Premium follows validation; Prestige remains future/limited.

---

## 2026-08 — Product Truth Hierarchy

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** Product authority follows: `Approved Product Specification → Approved Product Decision → Validated Research / Insight → Working Hypothesis → Archived Material`.

**Reason:** Prevents old research and founder assumptions from being mistaken for current product truth.

**Impact:** No component becomes final merely because it appeared in an earlier research document.

---

## 2026-08 — Research Architecture

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** Research is separated from strategy, decisions, and execution.

**Reason:** LAXEE needs to distinguish evidence from interpretation and choices.

**Impact:** New evidence goes into `02 Research`; strategic choices go to the relevant strategy or decision layer; product definitions belong in `03 Products`.
