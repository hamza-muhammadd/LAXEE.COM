# LAXEE — Key Decisions

This is the decision log for major LAXEE decisions.

## How to Record a Decision

For each important decision, record:

- **Date**
- **Decision**
- **Status** — Proposed / Active / Replaced
- **Reason**
- **Evidence**
- **Impact**

## Active Decisions

### 2026-08 — LAXEE as a Premium Gifting House

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** LAXEE will be positioned as a premium gifting house rather than a conventional gift shop.

**Reason:** This gives the brand room to compete through curation, presentation, personalization, service, and experience rather than through product volume alone.

**Evidence:** Brand strategy and market-category work.

**Impact:** Product, packaging, website, customer experience, and future retail decisions should reinforce the gifting-house model.

---

### 2026-08 — Quiet Opulence

**Status:** Active

**Decision:** “Quiet Opulence” is the current LAXEE brand motto and the guiding aesthetic principle.

**Reason:** LAXEE's intended premium position depends on restraint, quality, intentionality, and emotional sophistication rather than visual excess.

**Impact:** Brand identity, packaging, photography, digital design, and communication should be evaluated against this principle.
