# LAXEE — Current Goals

This document contains current company-level priorities. It should change as the business moves forward.

## Current Phase

**Foundation → Strategy → Product Development**

## Priority 01 — Establish the Brand System

Create a clear and usable brand foundation covering positioning, identity, voice, and customer experience principles.

**Success condition:** The brand can be applied consistently across product, packaging, website, and communication.

## Priority 02 — Validate the Market

Strengthen market, customer, and competitor research before making major assumptions about demand, pricing, and positioning.

**Success condition:** Major strategic claims are supported by evidence rather than intuition alone.

## Priority 03 — Build the First Product System

Turn the Nikah product family into clear, production-ready product definitions with consistent tiers, specifications, pricing logic, packaging, and customer experience.

**Success condition:** Each active product can move from concept to sourcing, production, sale, and fulfillment without ambiguity.

## Priority 04 — Develop the Packaging System

Define packaging as a repeatable LAXEE experience rather than treating each box as an isolated design.

**Success condition:** Materials, structure, visual identity, opening sequence, protection, and production requirements are documented.

## Priority 05 — Build the Commercial Foundation

Prepare the website, supplier network, operating procedures, and customer experience needed to support the first launch.

**Success condition:** A customer can discover, select, purchase, receive, and experience a LAXEE gift through a reliable end-to-end process.
