# 01. Purpose

Customer Experience (CX) Philosophy defines how LAXEE should make customers feel and how the brand should behave at every stage of the customer journey.

LAXEE does not view customer experience as simply:

- Customer service
- Website usability
- Delivery
- Packaging
- Communication

Instead:

> **Every interaction with LAXEE is part of the gift.**

The customer experience must therefore reflect the same values as the physical product:

**Thoughtfulness + Beauty + Ease + Trust + Meaning**

---

# 02. The LAXEE CX Philosophy

The central philosophy is:

> **Make giving feel effortless, personal, beautiful, and meaningful.**

The customer should never feel that they are simply completing a transaction.

They should feel that LAXEE is helping them create something special for someone.

---

# 03. The Desired Customer Feeling

At every major interaction, LAXEE should aim to create five primary feelings:

### 01. Understood

"I feel like LAXEE understands what I am trying to express."

### 02. Inspired

"LAXEE gives me ideas I would not have thought of myself."

### 03. Confident

"I trust that I am making the right choice."

### 04. Excited

"I cannot wait to give this."

### 05. Satisfied

"That was worth it."

These feelings should guide the design of the entire customer journey.

---

# 04. Customer Experience Principles

LAXEE's CX will be guided by eight principles.

---

## 4.1 Thoughtfulness First

Every customer interaction should demonstrate consideration.

LAXEE should anticipate customer needs rather than simply react to requests.

Examples:

- Gift recommendations
- Occasion suggestions
- Recipient-based recommendations
- Appropriate personalization
- Clear delivery expectations
- Helpful guidance

> **Thoughtfulness should be experienced, not advertised.**

---

## 4.2 Effortless Gifting

The customer should not have to manage unnecessary complexity.

LAXEE should simplify:

- Discovery
- Selection
- Customization
- Checkout
- Delivery
- Gift preparation

The desired experience is:

```text
"I have an idea."
        ↓
"LAXEE helps me."
        ↓
"I choose."
        ↓
"LAXEE handles the details."
        ↓
"I give."
