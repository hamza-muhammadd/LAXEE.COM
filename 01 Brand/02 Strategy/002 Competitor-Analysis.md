# LAXEE — Competitor Analysis

> **Strategic Objective:** Understand the competitive landscape, identify market gaps, and discover where LAXEE can build a distinctive and defensible position.

---

# 01. Purpose of Competitor Analysis

Competitor analysis is conducted to understand how existing brands compete within the gifting market and where opportunities exist for LAXEE to create a stronger and more differentiated position.

The objective is not simply to determine who is better.

The objective is to understand:

> **What is everyone already doing, and what is nobody doing exceptionally well?**

The findings from this analysis will directly inform:

- Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
- Value Proposition
- Premium Positioning
- Customer Experience Philosophy
- Product Architecture
- Brand Differentiation

---

# 02. Competitive Landscape

LAXEE's competitive landscape will be divided into three primary groups.

## 2.1 Direct Competitors

Direct competitors are brands that target similar customers and offer similar premium or curated gifting solutions.

Potential direct competitors include:

- Geeft
- The Gift Heaven
- Noorbi
- Other established premium gifting brands in Bangladesh

These brands will be evaluated based on their positioning, product range, pricing, curation, packaging, personalization, customer experience, and overall brand perception.

---

## 2.2 Indirect Competitors

Indirect competitors solve the same customer need through different products, business models, or experiences.

Examples include:

- Corporate gifting companies
- Flower and chocolate gifting businesses
- Personalized gift stores
- Social-media-based gift businesses
- Online marketplaces
- Premium lifestyle retailers
- Specialty retailers
- Occasion-specific gifting businesses

These businesses compete for the same customer's gifting budget even when their products differ from LAXEE.

---

## 2.3 Aspirational & Global Benchmarks

Aspirational competitors are not necessarily direct competitors.

They represent brands whose standards, systems, customer experiences, or global presence can serve as benchmarks for LAXEE's long-term ambition.

These may include:

- International gifting brands
- Luxury gifting houses
- Premium lifestyle brands
- Experience-based gifting platforms
- Global personalization platforms
- Premium retail brands

Aspirational benchmarks will help LAXEE understand what world-class execution looks like.

---

# 03. Competitor Evaluation Framework

Every relevant competitor will be evaluated using the same strategic framework.

| Dimension | What We Analyse |
|---|---|
| **Brand Positioning** | How the competitor defines itself |
| **Target Customer** | Who they primarily serve |
| **Market Category** | What category they compete within |
| **Product Range** | Products and categories offered |
| **Product Curation** | How products are selected and combined |
| **Pricing** | Entry, mid, premium, and luxury pricing |
| **Customization** | Personalization and custom gifting options |
| **Packaging** | Materials, aesthetics, presentation, and unboxing |
| **Visual Identity** | Logo, colors, typography, photography, design |
| **Brand Voice** | Communication style and messaging |
| **Customer Experience** | Discovery to delivery journey |
| **Digital Experience** | Website, social media, UX, ordering experience |
| **Occasion Coverage** | Birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, Eid, etc. |
| **Relationship Coverage** | For him, her, parents, friends, clients, etc. |
| **Corporate Gifting** | B2B gifting capabilities |
| **Physical Presence** | Stores, outlets, showrooms |
| **Delivery** | Coverage, speed, reliability, presentation |
| **Customer Service** | Support, communication, problem resolution |
| **Brand Trust** | Reviews, reputation, consistency |
| **Strengths** | Areas where the competitor performs strongly |
| **Weaknesses** | Areas where the competitor underperforms |
| **Market Gap** | Unmet needs or underserved opportunities |
| **LAXEE Opportunity** | Areas where LAXEE can differentiate |

# 04. Competitor Research Method

Competitor research should be based on observable evidence rather than assumptions.

Research sources may include:

- Official websites
- Social media platforms
- Product catalogues
- Pricing information
- Packaging examples
- Customer reviews
- Customer comments
- Advertisements
- Physical stores
- Online ordering experience
- Delivery experience
- Customer service interactions
- Publicly available business information

Where possible, competitor claims should be verified through multiple sources.

# 06. Competitive Dimensions

## 6.1 Product

Analyse:

- Product variety
- Product quality
- Product uniqueness
- Product sourcing
- Product combinations
- Product exclusivity
- Seasonal collections
- Occasion-based collections

### Key Question

> **Are competitors selling products, or are they creating thoughtfully curated gifts?**

---

## 6.2 Curation

Analyse how competitors combine products into complete gifting solutions.

Questions:

- Are products randomly bundled?
- Are collections thoughtfully themed?
- Is the recipient considered?
- Is the occasion considered?
- Is the emotional context considered?
- Can customers build their own gifts?

### Strategic Question

> **Can LAXEE make curation a core competitive advantage?**

---

## 6.3 Pricing

Analyse:

- Entry-level pricing
- Mid-range pricing
- Premium pricing
- Luxury pricing
- Customization charges
- Delivery charges
- Corporate pricing

The objective is not to become the cheapest option.

The objective is to understand:

> **What does the customer receive at each price level?**

---

## 6.4 Packaging

Analyse:

- Box quality
- Materials
- Color
- Typography
- Branding
- Finishing
- Protection
- Personalization
- Unboxing experience
- Reusability
- Sustainability

### Key Question

> **Does the packaging feel like part of the gift, or merely a container?**

---

## 6.5 Personalization

Analyse:

- Names
- Messages
- Cards
- Photos
- Custom products
- Custom packaging
- Gift combinations
- Recipient preferences

### Strategic Question

> **How can LAXEE make personalization feel premium rather than merely functional?**
