# LAXEE — Brand Voice

## 01 — Purpose

The LAXEE brand voice defines how the brand communicates with customers,
partners, and the wider audience.

LAXEE should sound as refined as it looks.

The language should communicate:

- Elegance
- Confidence
- Thoughtfulness
- Restraint
- Warmth
- Sophistication

---

# 02 — Core Voice

LAXEE speaks with:

### Quiet Confidence

We do not need to shout to communicate value.

### Refined Simplicity

We use fewer words, but make them meaningful.

### Thoughtful Warmth

We recognize the emotional significance behind gifting.

### Modern Sophistication

Our language is contemporary without being overly trendy.

### Intentionality

Every sentence should have a purpose.

---

# 03 — The LAXEE Voice

LAXEE should sound:

**Refined**  
Not overly casual.

**Warm**  
Not cold or distant.

**Confident**  
Not arrogant.

**Minimal**  
Not empty.

**Emotional**  
Not sentimental or dramatic.

**Premium**  
Not pretentious.

---

# 04 — Voice Formula

A useful formula for LAXEE communication:

> **Clarity + Emotion + Restraint**

Example:

Instead of:

> Make your loved one's special day unforgettable with our amazing
> premium gift box!!!

Use:

> A thoughtful gesture for a day worth remembering.

---

# 05 — Brand Vocabulary

Words that align with the LAXEE identity:

- Curated
- Thoughtful
- Refined
- Considered
- Meaningful
- Personal
- Crafted
- Elevated
- Timeless
- Beautiful
- Intentional
- Experience
- Moment
- Keepsake
- Occasion
- Detail
- Quiet
- Distinctive
- Exceptional

---

# 06 — Words to Avoid

Avoid language that makes LAXEE feel cheap, aggressive, or generic.

Examples:

- Cheap
- Cheapest
- Hurry
- Buy now!!!
- Crazy deal
- Best ever
- Amazing!!!
- Super cheap
- Must buy
- Viral
- Guaranteed luxury
- No.1 gift
- Limited!!! Hurry!!!
- Don't miss!!!

Promotional language may be used when necessary, but should remain
controlled and sophisticated.

---

# 07 — Sentence Style

Prefer:

- Short sentences
- Clear statements
- Strong nouns
- Precise adjectives
- Natural rhythm
- Meaningful pauses

Avoid:

- Long promotional paragraphs
- Excessive adjectives
- Repetition
- Over-explaining
- Excessive punctuation

---

# 08 — Punctuation

LAXEE communication should use punctuation with restraint.

### Preferred

> Thoughtfully curated. Quietly presented.

### Avoid

> The BEST gift EVER!!! 😍😍🔥🔥

Exclamation marks should be rare.

---

# 09 — Product Description Style

A LAXEE product description should answer:

1. What is it?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Who is it for?
4. What makes it distinctive?

### Example

Instead of:

> Our amazing Nikah gift box comes with lots of beautiful products
> that are perfect for the bride and groom!

Use:

> A thoughtfully curated collection created to mark the beginning
> of a new chapter.

---

# 10 — Website Voice

Website copy should be:

- Clear
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Informative
- Emotionally intelligent

### Example

**Heading:**

> Gifts, thoughtfully considered.

**Supporting copy:**

> Curated objects and experiences for the moments that matter.

**Call to Action:**

Preferred:

- Explore Collection
- Discover the Collection
- View the Collection
- Find Your Gift

Avoid:

- Buy Now!!!
- Grab Yours
- Shop Till You Drop
- Get It Before It's Gone

---

# 11 — Social Media Voice

Social media should feel more human while maintaining the LAXEE identity.

### Preferred

> Some moments deserve more than a simple gift.

### Preferred

> For the beginning of something beautiful.

### Avoid

> Guys!!! Look what we have for you 😍🔥

---

# 12 — Promotional Voice

When communicating offers or promotions, remain refined.

### Preferred

> A special selection, available for a limited time.

### Preferred

> Selected pieces from the collection, now available.

### Avoid

> HUGE DISCOUNT!!! BUY NOW!!!

---

# 13 — Packaging Voice

Packaging copy should be extremely concise.

Examples:

> Thoughtfully chosen for you.

> A little something, meaningfully chosen.

> For a moment worth remembering.

> With thought, from LAXEE.

> Quietly yours.

Packaging should not contain unnecessary marketing language.

---

# 14 — Customer Service Voice

Customer service should be:

- Respectful
- Clear
- Calm
- Helpful
- Human

### Example

Instead of:

> We can't do that.

Use:

> We're unable to offer that option at the moment, but we'd be happy
> to suggest an alternative.

Instead of:

> Your order is late.

Use:

> Your order is taking a little longer than expected. We appreciate
> your patience and will keep you updated.

---

# 15 — Premium Customer Communication

For high-value or bespoke customers, communication should feel personal
rather than automated.

### Example

> We'd be pleased to curate this specifically for the occasion.

Instead of:

> Select from the options below.

---

# 16 — Emotional Language

LAXEE can use emotion, but should avoid excessive sentimentality.

### Preferred

> For the beginning of a new chapter.

> A thoughtful way to mark the moment.

> Made for moments worth remembering.

### Avoid

> Show them how much you REALLY REALLY love them!!!

---

# 17 — Luxury Language

Luxury should be communicated through confidence and precision.

### Preferred

> Carefully selected.

> Crafted with intention.

> Considered in every detail.

> Designed to be remembered.

### Avoid

> The most luxurious gift in Bangladesh!!!

---

# 18 — Multilingual Communication

LAXEE may communicate in:

- English
- Bangla
- Arabic

The underlying brand personality should remain consistent across languages.

Translation should preserve:

- Meaning
- Tone
- Elegance
- Emotional nuance

Literal translation should be avoided when it makes the communication
sound unnatural.

---

# 19 — Bangla Voice

Bangla communication should feel:

- Natural
- Sophisticated
- Modern
- Warm
- Respectful

Avoid overly formal or artificially literary language when communicating
with customers.

### Preferred Direction

> একটি অর্থবহ মুহূর্তের জন্য, যত্নে নির্বাচিত।

Rather than overly promotional language.

---

# 20 — Arabic Voice

Arabic communication should maintain the same principles of refinement,
clarity, and warmth.

Arabic copy should be reviewed for:

- Linguistic accuracy
- Cultural appropriateness
- Natural phrasing
- Consistency with LAXEE's identity

---

# 21 — Tone by Situation

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Website | Refined + Clear |
| Product Description | Thoughtful + Descriptive |
| Instagram | Elegant + Human |
| Advertisement | Confident + Concise |
| Customer Service | Warm + Professional |
| Packaging | Minimal + Emotional |
| Corporate | Sophisticated + Precise |
| Prestige Collection | Exclusive + Bespoke |

---

# 22 — The LAXEE Test

Before publishing any communication, ask:

### Does it sound like LAXEE?

### Is it clear?

### Is it necessary?

### Does it feel refined?

### Does it communicate value without shouting?

### Does it feel human?

### Does it support "Quiet Opulence"?

If not, rewrite it.

---

# 23 — Voice Principle

> Say less.
>
> Mean more.
>
> Make every word intentional.
