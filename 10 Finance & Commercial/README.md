# LAXEE — Finance & Commercial

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Create financial clarity around products, pricing, costs, margins, cash requirements, and commercial decisions.

## Sections

- `Unit-Economics/` — cost, contribution margin, and economics by product
- `Pricing/` — pricing architecture and pricing decisions
- `Costs/` — fixed and variable cost structure
- `Revenue/` — revenue planning and performance
- `Budget/` — budgets and financial plans
- `Commercial-Planning/` — commercial scenarios and targets

## Product Economics

Every active product should eventually have visibility into:

`Selling Price → Variable Cost → Contribution → Packaging → Fulfillment → Acquisition Cost → Net Contribution`

## Rule

Pricing is a commercial decision informed by brand positioning, customer value, competitive evidence, and actual unit economics.
