# LAXEE — People & Governance

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Define how responsibility, decision rights, collaboration, hiring, and company culture work as LAXEE grows.

## Sections

- `Roles/` — roles and responsibilities
- `Responsibilities/` — ownership matrix and accountability
- `Hiring/` — hiring principles and role briefs
- `Meetings/` — meeting rhythms and agendas
- `Decision-Rights/` — who can decide what
- `Culture/` — internal culture and behavioral principles

## Principle

Ownership should be explicit. A task can have many contributors, but an important outcome should have one accountable owner.

## Governance Rule

Major strategic decisions should be recorded in `00 Company/Key-Decisions.md`.
