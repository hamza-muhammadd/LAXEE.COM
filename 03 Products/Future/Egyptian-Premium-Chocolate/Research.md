# LAXEE-PR-001 — Egyptian Premium Chocolate

**Status:** Initial Validation — TEST ONLY  
**Source:** Legacy product research, consolidated

## Concept

A 100g premium Egyptian chocolate that could serve as a component inside LAXEE curated collections, including Nikah, Eid/Ramadan, anniversary, corporate, and general gifting.

## Why It Is Interesting

- Familiar gifting category.
- Shareable and celebration-oriented.
- Can support perceived value inside curated boxes.
- Egyptian origin may provide differentiation if genuinely scarce in Bangladesh.

## What Is Still Unverified

The original research explicitly marks the following as assumptions requiring verification:

- Exact Bangladesh availability.
- Number of comparable sellers.
- Retail price.
- Product quality.
- Packaging quality.
- Customer reviews.
- Brand recognition.
- Import availability.
- Supplier identity.
- MOQ.
- Unit price.
- Shipping.
- Duties/taxes.
- Final landed cost.

## Commercial Rule

No financial estimate from the legacy document should be treated as a purchasing quote or final unit economics.

Actual supplier quotation, shipping, customs/duties, taxes, spoilage risk, and storage must be verified.

## Current Decision

**TEST — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**

The product should only enter an active LAXEE collection after evidence and sourcing validation.
