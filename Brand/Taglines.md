# LAXEE — Tagline System

## 01 — Purpose

The LAXEE tagline system defines the short statements used to express
the brand, collections, campaigns, and gifting experiences.

Taglines should remain:

- Elegant
- Memorable
- Concise
- Meaningful
- Distinctive
- Consistent with the LAXEE identity

---

# 02 — Primary Brand Motto

## Quiet Opulence

**Status:** Primary / Approved

This is the central expression of the LAXEE brand philosophy.

It represents the idea that true luxury can be expressed through
restraint, intention, quality, and detail rather than excess.
