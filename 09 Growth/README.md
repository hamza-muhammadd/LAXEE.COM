# LAXEE — Growth

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Build a measurable system for attracting the right customers, creating demand, building relationships, and growing revenue without weakening the brand.

## Sections

- `Marketing/` — strategy and channel planning
- `Content/` — content system and editorial planning
- `Social/` — social channels and community
- `Campaigns/` — campaign briefs, launches, and post-mortems
- `Partnerships/` — strategic partnerships and collaborations
- `CRM/` — customer relationship and retention
- `Analytics/` — growth metrics and learning

## Principle

Growth should amplify the LAXEE brand rather than make the brand louder than it needs to be.

## Campaign Lifecycle

`Insight → Objective → Audience → Proposition → Creative → Launch → Measure → Learn → Archive`
