# LAXEE — Supply Chain

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

This section manages the supply network required to reliably create and deliver LAXEE products.

## Sections

- `Suppliers/` — approved and prospective suppliers
- `Sourcing/` — sourcing research and procurement work
- `Manufacturing/` — production partners and capabilities
- `Materials/` — material sourcing and specifications
- `Logistics/` — movement, storage, and delivery requirements
- `Quality/` — supplier and incoming-quality standards
- `Evaluation/` — supplier assessment and approval records

## Supplier Lifecycle

`Discovered → Contacted → Sampled → Evaluated → Approved → Active → Paused → Rejected`

## Supplier Record Standard

Record, where available:

- Company
- Category
- Location
- Contact
- Capabilities
- MOQ
- Pricing
- Lead time
- Capacity
- Quality
- Samples
- Payment terms
- Reliability
- Risks
- Last verified date
- Status

## Rule

Supplier research is evidence. Supplier approval is a decision. Active supplier relationships belong here.
