# NIKAH-001 — Supplier & Sourcing Research

**Status:** Framework established — supplier validation not completed

## Objective

Identify reliable sourcing options for selected product components.

## Supplier Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Product Quality | 25% |
| Unit Cost | 20% |
| Customization | 15% |
| Reliability | 15% |
| MOQ | 10% |
| Lead Time | 5% |
| Availability | 5% |
| Packaging Compatibility | 5% |

## Supplier Lifecycle

`Discovered → Contacted → Sampled → Evaluated → Approved → Active`

## Required Supplier Evidence

- Unit cost
- MOQ
- Customization capability
- Lead time
- Sample quality
- Consistency
- Payment terms
- Availability
- Replacement policy
- Packaging compatibility
- Primary/backup status

## Quality Rule

Do not select the cheapest supplier automatically.

> **Quality → Reliability → Product Fit → Cost**

## Sample Gate

Before approval, inspect physical quality, size, color, material, finish, durability, personalization quality, presentation, and consistency.
