# LAXEE — Packaging System

**Status:** Active

Packaging is part of the LAXEE product experience, not simply a container.

## Purpose

Define how every LAXEE package is conceived, designed, sourced, produced, checked, and experienced.

## Sections

- `Strategy/` — role of packaging in the brand and product system
- `Concepts/` — creative directions and explorations
- `Structure/` — dimensions, construction, dielines, mechanisms
- `Materials/` — boards, fabrics, finishes, inserts, protective materials
- `Artwork/` — visual application and production-ready artwork
- `Production/` — manufacturing requirements and production records
- `Quality/` — inspection standards and acceptance criteria
- `Experience/` — opening sequence, presentation, personalization, sensory details

## Packaging Lifecycle

`Concept → Structural Prototype → Visual Prototype → Sample → Validated → Production → Active → Retired`

## Rule

Every packaging decision should balance four things:

1. Brand expression
2. Product protection
3. Customer experience
4. Commercial and production reality
