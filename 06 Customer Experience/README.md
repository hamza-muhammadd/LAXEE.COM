# LAXEE — Customer Experience

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Define the complete customer experience from discovery to post-purchase relationship.

## Experience System

```text
Discover
  ↓
Understand
  ↓
Choose
  ↓
Personalize
  ↓
Purchase
  ↓
Anticipate
  ↓
Receive
  ↓
Unbox
  ↓
Gift
  ↓
Remember
  ↓
Return / Recommend
```

## Sections

- `Customer-Journey/` — end-to-end journey and touchpoints
- `Purchase/` — buying experience
- `Personalization/` — meaningful customization
- `Delivery/` — delivery experience and expectations
- `Unboxing/` — physical experience
- `Customer-Service/` — service principles and standards
- `Feedback/` — feedback, complaints, reviews, and learning

## Principle

LAXEE should design the experience around the recipient and the giver, not only around the product.
