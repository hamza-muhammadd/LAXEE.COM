# LAXEE — Market & Research

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Research is the evidence layer of LAXEE. It records what we observe, measure, test, and learn before findings become strategic decisions or operating standards.

## Research Architecture

```text
Evidence
   ↓
Research Record
   ↓
Synthesis / Insight
   ↓
Hypothesis
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Strategy / Product / Operations
```

## Sections

- `Market/` — market size, category dynamics, trends, demand
- `Customers/` — customer profiles, needs, interviews, behavior, feedback
- `Competitors/` — competitor evidence and benchmarking
- `Category/` — gifting categories, occasions, substitutes, standards
- `Product/` — product-level research and validation
- `Pricing/` — price research, willingness-to-pay, benchmarks
- `Suppliers/` — sourcing research before supplier approval
- `Insights/` — synthesized findings and implications

## Research Record Standard

Where practical, every meaningful research record should identify:

- Research question
- Objective
- Source
- Date
- Observation / evidence
- Interpretation
- Confidence
- Limitations
- Implication
- Next action

## Evidence Discipline

Use these evidence states:

- **Observed** — directly seen or measured
- **Supported** — supported by multiple credible sources
- **Hypothesis** — plausible but unproven
- **Validated** — tested sufficiently for the current decision
- **Invalidated** — contradicted by evidence

Never present assumptions as facts.

## Research vs Strategy

Research answers:

> **What do we know?**

Strategy answers:

> **What does LAXEE choose to do about it?**

Major strategic choices should be recorded in `00 Company/Key-Decisions.md` and reflected in the appropriate Brand, Product, Customer Experience, or Operations document.

## Research vs Projects

Research is the knowledge system. Active research work is managed as a project when it has a defined owner, scope, deadline, and deliverable.

## Legacy Migration

The old top-level `Research/` area is being retired in favor of this structure. Valuable existing research should be reviewed and migrated; duplicate, empty, speculative, or obsolete material should be deleted or archived rather than copied blindly.
