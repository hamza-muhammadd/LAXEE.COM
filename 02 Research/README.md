# LAXEE — Market & Research

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Research is the evidence layer of LAXEE. It records what we observe, measure, test, and learn before those findings become strategic decisions.

## Sections

- `Market/` — market size, category dynamics, trends, demand
- `Customers/` — customer profiles, needs, behavior, interviews, feedback
- `Competitors/` — competitor evidence and benchmarking
- `Category/` — gifting category, occasions, substitutes, standards
- `Product/` — product-level research and validation
- `Pricing/` — price research, willingness-to-pay, benchmarks
- `Suppliers/` — sourcing research before suppliers become approved records
- `Insights/` — synthesized findings and implications

## Research Record Standard

Where practical, every meaningful research record should identify:

- Source
- Date
- Question
- Observation
- Evidence
- Interpretation
- Confidence
- Implication

## Critical Rule

Research is not automatically truth. Separate **evidence**, **interpretation**, and **hypothesis**.

A strategic decision should point back to the evidence that informed it.
