# NIKAH-001 — Customer Research

**Status:** Hypothesis Stage — Validation Required  
**Market:** Bangladesh / potential diaspora

## Core Question

Who buys a premium Nikah gift, why do they buy it, and what would make them choose LAXEE?

## Buyer vs Recipient

The buyer and recipient may be different people.

### Potential Buyers

Friends, siblings, relatives, parents/families, colleagues, couples, and diaspora customers.

### Potential Recipients

Bride, groom, or newly married couple.

## Customer Jobs

The research identifies five major jobs:

- **Functional:** find an appropriate Nikah gift.
- **Emotional:** express care, respect, congratulations, or dua.
- **Social:** give something appropriate and impressive.
- **Convenience:** avoid assembling multiple gifts.
- **Memory:** create a gift that is remembered.

## Initial Pain-Point Hypotheses

- Too many choices.
- Generic gifts.
- Difficulty judging quality online.
- Weak presentation.
- Limited meaningful personalization.
- Time required to assemble gifts.
- Delivery uncertainty.
- Difficulty finding specialized premium Nikah gifts.

These are hypotheses until validated.

## Expected Value Drivers

Potential drivers include product quality, presentation, relevance, personalization, convenience, trust, emotional meaning, uniqueness, unboxing, and delivery reliability.

The existing research correctly identifies that these should be **ranked by customer evidence rather than assumed**.

## Purchase Triggers

Upcoming Nikah/wedding, close relationship with the couple, desire for a memorable gift, personalization, social expectations, and last-minute gifting.

## Purchase Barriers

Price, trust, product relevance, preference for individual gifts, delivery risk, and insufficient customization.

## Willingness to Pay

The original research uses ৳1,500–৳3,000, ৳3,000–৳7,000, and ৳7,000+ as working bands. These are **research ranges, not validated LAXEE pricing**.

## Research Plan

### Quantitative

Online survey: target 30 minimum, preferably 50–100+ responses.

### Qualitative

5–10 initial interviews with likely buyers.

### Behavioral Evidence

Competitor reviews, comments, questions, gifting discussions, and observed purchase behavior.

## Critical Questions

1. What is normally gifted at Nikah?
2. What is normally spent?
3. What products feel appropriate?
4. Fixed curated box or customization?
5. Does personalization increase willingness to pay?
6. What makes packaging feel premium?
7. What makes a gift feel cheap?
8. What price feels valuable versus excessive?
9. What should the recipient feel?
10. What creates memorability?

## Current Persona Hypothesis

**The Thoughtful Gift Buyer:** a relationship-close buyer who wants a meaningful, premium, presentable gift without spending hours assembling one.

## Decision

**CUSTOMER UNDERSTANDING: HYPOTHESIS ONLY — VALIDATION REQUIRED**

The original document should be treated as a research plan plus hypotheses, not customer facts.
