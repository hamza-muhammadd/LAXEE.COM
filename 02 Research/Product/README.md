# Product Research

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Product research determines whether a potential product deserves to become part of the LAXEE product system.

Research belongs here until a decision is made. Once a product is approved, its production definition belongs in `03 Products`.

## Product Research Lifecycle

```text
IDEA
  ↓
DISCOVERY
  ↓
MARKET CHECK
  ↓
CUSTOMER CHECK
  ↓
COMPETITOR CHECK
  ↓
SUPPLIER CHECK
  ↓
COST / PRICING CHECK
  ↓
BRAND FIT
  ↓
PRODUCT SCORE
  ↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
  ↓
TEST / SAMPLE
  ↓
DECISION
  ↓
PROCEED / REFINE / HOLD / REJECT
```

## Product Research Record

Every serious candidate should eventually record:

- Product ID
- Product name
- Category
- Occasion
- Customer problem / job
- Market evidence
- Competitive evidence
- Product quality
- Supplier options
- Cost and price evidence
- Brand fit
- Differentiation
- Risks
- Validation requirements
- Recommendation
- Status

## Numbering

Use a permanent Product Research ID when a candidate becomes a serious research record.

Format:

`LAXEE-PR-001`

Do not reuse IDs.

## Decision Rule

A product is not approved because it is attractive, popular, available, or inexpensive.

It must demonstrate a credible combination of:

**Customer Value + Brand Fit + Quality + Differentiation + Feasibility + Commercial Viability**
