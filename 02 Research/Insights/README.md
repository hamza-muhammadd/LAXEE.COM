# Research Insights

**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Insights are synthesized findings created from multiple pieces of evidence. They are not raw research and they are not yet company decisions.

## Insight Standard

Every important insight should answer:

1. What evidence supports this?
2. What pattern are we seeing?
3. How confident are we?
4. What could invalidate it?
5. What decision or experiment could it inform?

## Evidence Levels

- **Observed** — directly seen or measured
- **Supported** — supported by multiple credible sources
- **Hypothesis** — plausible but not yet validated
- **Validated** — tested sufficiently for the current decision
- **Invalidated** — evidence contradicts the assumption

## Rule

Never turn a hypothesis into a brand or product truth without recording the evidence and decision separately.
