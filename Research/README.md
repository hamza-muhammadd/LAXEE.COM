# LAXEE — Research

## Purpose

This section contains research used to support LAXEE product,
market, competitor, and supplier decisions.

Research should be documented before major product or purchasing
decisions are made.

---

# Research Principles

LAXEE research should be:

- Evidence-based
- Organized
- Current
- Source-backed
- Commercially relevant
- Easy to verify

Avoid making business decisions based only on assumptions or opinions.

# Research Record Standard

Every significant research entry should document:

- Research date
- Research question
- Objective
- Findings
- Sources
- Price information
- Market observations
- Opportunities
- Risks
- Recommendation
- Next action

---

# Source Quality

Prioritize:

1. Official supplier information
2. Official company websites
3. Direct quotations
4. Marketplace data
5. Customer reviews
6. Industry reports
7. Social media observations

Clearly distinguish verified information from assumptions.

---

# Decision Principle

Research exists to support decisions.

A research document should ideally answer:

> What did we discover?

> What does it mean?

> What should LAXEE do next?
