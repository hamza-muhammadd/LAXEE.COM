# 03. Competitive Landscape

The initial competitive landscape contains several business models.

## Model 01 — Custom Gift Box

Customers select products and create their own box.

**Example:** C-001 Noorbi

Noorbi currently provides a multi-step custom gift-box builder where customers select a box, add gifts, choose styling and add a card/message.

---

## Model 02 — Curated Personalized Gifting

The brand curates a collection of products around the customer's occasion and allows personalization.

**Example:** C-002 Deenbz

Deenbz offers curated gifts, personalization and a build-your-own gift-box experience.

---

## Model 03 — Custom Luxury Hampers

The brand designs a hamper based on customer requirements.

**Example:** C-003 Tanukrit

Tanukrit describes a process of gathering requirements, curating the hamper, approving personalization and then packing/delivering it.

---

## Model 04 — Premium Curated Hampers

The brand offers ready-made premium hampers at different price points.

**Example:** C-004 Chondon Express

Its current product selection includes hampers from approximately ৳4,050 to ৳12,200.

---

## Model 05 — Occasion-Based Online Gifting

The brand offers products for multiple occasions, including weddings and engagements.

**Example:** C-005 UpoharBD

UpoharBD maintains a dedicated wedding-gift category and offers a broad range of wedding and engagement gifts.

# 05. C-001 — Noorbi

## Positioning

Noorbi presents itself as a premium gift shop offering curated gift packages, custom gift boxes, flowers, chocolates and personalized gifts.

## Product Model

Its custom box system allows customers to:

1. Select box
2. Add gifts
3. Select styling
4. Add card/message
5. Review order

It also offers a Wedding Gifts Combo at ৳6,670.

## Strengths

* Strong customization
* Clear purchasing process
* Multiple product categories
* Wedding-specific offering
* Clear online ordering
* Multiple box sizes

## Potential Weaknesses / Areas to Investigate

* Degree of true luxury positioning
* Consistency of product curation
* Emotional storytelling
* Nikah-specific product philosophy
* Distinctive brand identity
* Premium packaging depth

## LAXEE Opportunity

LAXEE should investigate whether it can create a more deeply curated and emotionally specific Nikah experience rather than simply allowing customers to assemble products.

# 10. Competitive Patterns

The initial analysis reveals several recurring patterns.

## Pattern 01 — Customization

Multiple competitors allow customers to personalize or build gift boxes.

Therefore:

> **Customization alone is unlikely to be a sufficient USP.**

---

## Pattern 02 — Curated Hampers

Curated hampers are already an established product format.

Therefore:

> LAXEE cannot simply sell "a hamper."

The curation itself must be distinctive.

---

## Pattern 03 — Premium Packaging

Premium packaging is already used as a selling point.

Therefore:

> Packaging quality is necessary but may not be sufficient as the sole differentiator.

---

## Pattern 04 — Emotional Messaging

Competitors already use emotional language around love, care, meaning and thoughtfulness.

Therefore:

> LAXEE needs to translate emotional positioning into an actual product experience, not only marketing copy.

---

## Pattern 05 — Occasion-Based Gifting

Competitors cover birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and other occasions.

Therefore:

> LAXEE's opportunity may be stronger if it creates highly intentional occasion-specific products.

# 17. Preliminary Conclusion

## Competitive Status

**HIGH COMPETITION — BUT POTENTIAL DIFFERENTIATION EXISTS**

LAXEE is entering an existing gifting market rather than creating a new category.

However, the current landscape appears fragmented across:

* General gifting
* Personalized gifting
* Custom gift boxes
* Premium hampers
* Wedding gifting

This may create an opportunity for LAXEE to establish a more focused position around:

> **Premium, curated, meaningful Nikah gifting.**

The opportunity is not yet proven.

The next stages must determine whether customers actually value this differentiation and whether the product can be produced at a commercially viable margin.
