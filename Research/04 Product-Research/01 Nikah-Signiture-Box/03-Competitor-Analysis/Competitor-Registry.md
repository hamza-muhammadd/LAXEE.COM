# 01. Purpose

This registry records all relevant competitors identified during the research of the LAXEE Nikah Signature Box.

Each competitor receives a permanent ID so that the competitor can be referenced consistently throughout the LAXEE research and product-development system.

---

# 02. Competitor Classification

## Direct Competitor

A business offering products substantially similar to a premium Nikah / wedding gift box or curated gift hamper.

## Indirect Competitor

A business selling premium, personalized, or curated gifts that could substitute for a Nikah Signature Box.

## Substitute

A product or service customers may purchase instead of a curated gift box.

# 07. Competitor Expansion Rule

The registry should remain open.

New competitors discovered during:

* Market research
* Customer research
* Product research
* Social media research
* Google search
* Marketplace research
* Customer interviews

must receive the next available ID.

Example:

`C-006`, `C-007`, `C-008`...

Competitor IDs must never be reused.

---

# 08. Research Principle

A competitor should not be considered important simply because it sells gifts.

Relevance will be determined by:

1. Product similarity
2. Target customer similarity
3. Price similarity
4. Occasion similarity
5. Premium positioning
6. Customization
7. Gift-box / hamper capability
