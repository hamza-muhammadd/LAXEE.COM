## Objective

To research, validate, develop, price, package, and prepare the LAXEE Nikah Signature Box for launch.

The objective is not simply to assemble a collection of products, but to develop a distinctive, meaningful, and premium LAXEE gifting experience.

---

## Research & Development Flow

1. Market Analysis
2. Customer Research
3. Competitor Analysis
4. Product Selection
   - Candidate Discovery
   - Candidate Screening
   - Product Scoring
   - Shortlisting
5. Product Research & Validation
6. Pricing & Unit Economics
7. Supplier & Sourcing Research
8. Packaging Research
9. Final Validation
10. Final Product Decision
11. Product Development
12. Launch Preparation

---

## Product Selection Framework

The Signature Box may include elements such as:

- Hero Product
- Nikah / Islamic Element
- Couple / Emotional Element
- Personal Luxury
- Gourmet / Consumable
- Keepsake
- Experience Element
- Presentation Element

These are design and curation lenses, not mandatory product quantities.

---

## Decision Framework

Every shortlisted product and the final box will be evaluated against:

- Customer Value
- Nikah Relevance
- Premium Perception
- LAXEE Brand Fit
- Emotional Value
- Product Quality
- Market Demand
- Competitive Position
- Sourcing Feasibility
- Unit Economics
- Differentiation Potential

---

## Final Outcome

The project will end with one of three decisions:

### GO
Proceed to Product Development.

### REVISE
Modify the concept and return to the relevant research stage.

### REJECT
Do not proceed with the product concept.
