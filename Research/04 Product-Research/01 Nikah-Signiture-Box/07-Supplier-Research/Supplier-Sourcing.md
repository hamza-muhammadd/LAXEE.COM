# LAXEÈ — Nikah Signature Box
# Supplier & Sourcing Research

**Project ID:** NIKAH-001  
**Research Phase:** Supplier & Sourcing Research  
**Status:** In Progress  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## 01 — Objective

To identify, evaluate, and validate suitable suppliers and sourcing options for the LAXEÈ Nikah Signature Box.

The research will determine:

- Supplier availability
- Product quality
- Unit cost
- MOQ
- Customization capability
- Lead time
- Reliability
- Packaging compatibility
- Backup sourcing options

---

## 02 — Selected Product Framework

Supplier research will be conducted against the products already shortlisted during Product Selection.

No new product will be added solely because a supplier offers it.

Any new candidate must go through the established Product Selection process.

---

## 03 — Supplier Requirements

Every potential supplier should be evaluated against:

### Product Quality
- Material quality
- Finish
- Durability
- Appearance
- Consistency

### Commercial
- Unit price
- Wholesale price
- MOQ
- Bulk discount
- Payment terms

### Customization
- Engraving
- Printing
- Branding
- Color options
- Packaging options
- Personalization

### Operations
- Lead time
- Production capacity
- Availability
- Delivery
- Replacement policy

### Reliability
- Supplier history
- Communication
- Sample quality
- Order consistency

**1 = Very Poor**

to

**5 = Excellent**

# 08 — Sample Validation

Before approving a supplier for critical Signature Box components, samples should be evaluated where practical.

### Sample Checklist

- [ ] Physical quality
- [ ] Size accuracy
- [ ] Color accuracy
- [ ] Material quality
- [ ] Finish
- [ ] Durability
- [ ] Packaging
- [ ] Personalization quality
- [ ] Photograph / presentation quality
- [ ] Consistency with LAXEÈ standards

---

# 09 — Supplier Risk Assessment

Potential risks:

### Supply Risk
- Stock unavailable
- Supplier discontinuation
- Seasonal availability

### Quality Risk
- Inconsistent quality
- Damaged products
- Color variation
- Poor finishing

### Commercial Risk
- Price changes
- High MOQ
- Hidden costs

### Operational Risk
- Delayed production
- Delayed delivery
- Poor communication

### Customization Risk
- Inconsistent engraving
- Printing defects
- Personalization errors

---

# 10 — Primary / Backup Supplier Strategy

Critical products should ideally have:

**Primary Supplier**

and, where practical:

**Backup Supplier**

This reduces dependency on a single supplier.

---

# 11 — Sourcing Decision Rules

A supplier should not be selected solely because it offers the lowest price.

Priority:

> **Quality → Reliability → Product Fit → Cost**

A cheaper supplier that compromises the Signature Box experience should be rejected.

---

# 12 — Final Supplier Decision

For every product:

### Supplier Status

- **APPROVED**
- **CONDITIONAL**
- **REJECTED**
- **PENDING SAMPLE**

- [ ] Lead time confirmed

- [ ] Major risks documented
