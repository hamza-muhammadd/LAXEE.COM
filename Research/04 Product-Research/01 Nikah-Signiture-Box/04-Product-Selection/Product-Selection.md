# LAXEE — Product Selection

## Product Research Project

**Project ID:** NIKAH-001  
**Product:** Nikah Signature Box  
**Market:** Bangladesh  
**Stage:** 04 — Product Selection  
**Status:** In Progress  
**Started:** 08 August 2026

---

# 01. Purpose

The purpose of this stage is to determine what products should be included in the LAXEE Nikah Signature Box.

LAXEE will not select products simply because they look attractive or are popular.

Every product must have a clear role in creating the overall:

> **Meaning + Emotion + Luxury + Delight + Memory + Experience**

### Core Principle

> **Every product must have a reason to exist.**

---

# 02. Product Composition Framework

The Nikah Signature Box will be designed around eight product roles.

## 01 — Hero Product

The main attraction of the box.

The product that should be most memorable and potentially become a signature element of the LAXEE experience.

---

## 02 — Nikah / Islamic Element

The element that connects the gift with the meaning and identity of Nikah.

Examples may include:

- Quran
- Tasbih
- Dua card
- Islamic book
- Nikah keepsake
- Islamic artwork

---

## 03 — Couple / Emotional Element

The element that communicates love, connection and personal sentiment.

Examples may include:

- Personalized letter
- Couple card
- Custom artwork
- Couple journal
- Personalized message

---

## 04 — Personal Luxury

A refined personal item that makes the recipient feel valued.

Examples may include:

- Premium perfume
- Attar
- Premium candle
- Luxury accessory
- Personal care item

---

## 05 — Gourmet / Consumable

An enjoyable sensory element.

Examples may include:

- Premium chocolate
- Dates
- Nuts
- Specialty confectionery
- Tea / coffee

---

## 06 — Keepsake

An item intended to remain as a long-term memory of the occasion.

Examples may include:

- Engraved item
- Couple keepsake
- Personalized artwork
- Couple journal
- Decorative object

---

## 07 — Experience Element

An element designed specifically to make receiving and opening the gift memorable.

Examples may include:

- Reveal card
- Hidden message
- Product story card
- Layered unboxing
- Special opening mechanism

---

## 08 — Presentation Element

The physical and visual system that turns the products into a LAXEE experience.

Examples may include:

- Signature box
- Inner tray
- Tissue
- Ribbon
- Greeting card
- Product cards
- Brand card

## 04. Candidate Screening

Candidate screening determines which discovered products deserve further evaluation.

At this stage, we are NOT approving final products.

Each candidate is evaluated against:

1. Nikah Fit
2. Emotional Value
3. Premium Feel
4. LAXEE Fit
5. Box Fit
6. Feasibility

### Decision Labels

- **KEEP** — Strong candidate; proceed to deeper evaluation.
- **MAYBE** — Potential candidate; retain for consideration but lower priority.
- **REJECT** — Does not justify further consideration for this Signature Box.

> KEEP does not mean APPROVED.

The screening stage does not determine the final box composition.

**KEEP** → Proceed to scoring.

**MAYBE** → Retain as secondary options.

**REJECT** → Remove from active consideration.

## 05. Product Scoring

Product scoring is used to objectively compare candidates that passed the initial screening stage.

Scoring does not automatically approve a product.

### Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Nikah Fit | 20% |
| Emotional Value | 20% |
| Premium Perception | 20% |
| LAXEE Fit | 15% |
| Box Fit | 15% |
| Feasibility | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Very Weak |
| 2 | Weak |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Strong |
| 5 | Exceptional |

### Weighted Score Formula

Final Score =
(Nikah Fit × 20%)
+ (Emotional Value × 20%)
+ (Premium Perception × 20%)
+ (LAXEE Fit × 15%)
+ (Box Fit × 15%)
+ (Feasibility × 10%)

### Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 4.50–5.00 | Exceptional Candidate |
| 4.00–4.49 | Strong Candidate |
| 3.50–3.99 | Promising Candidate |
| 3.00–3.49 | Weak Candidate |
| Below 3.00 | Low Priority |

> Scores are decision-support tools, not automatic approval criteria.
