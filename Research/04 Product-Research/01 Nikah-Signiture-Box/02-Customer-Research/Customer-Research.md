# LAXEE — Customer Research

## Product Research Project

**Project ID:** NIKAH-001  
**Product:** Nikah Signature Box  
**Research Stage:** 02 — Customer Research  
**Market:** Bangladesh  
**Status:** In Progress  

---

# 01. Research Objective

The purpose of this research is to understand the people who may purchase or receive the LAXEE Nikah Signature Box.

This research will identify:

- Who the buyer is
- Who the recipient is
- Why they buy
- When they buy
- What they expect
- What they value
- What they dislike
- What they are willing to spend
- What makes a gift feel premium
- What may prevent a purchase

### Core Question

> **Who will buy the LAXEE Nikah Signature Box, why will they buy it, and what would make them choose LAXEE?**

---

# 02. Buyer vs Recipient

The person purchasing the product may not be the person receiving it.

Therefore, these two groups must be analysed separately.

## Buyer

Potential buyers may include:

- Friends of the couple
- Siblings
- Cousins
- Relatives
- Parents
- Family members
- Colleagues
- Close acquaintances
- Couples
- Overseas / diaspora customers

## Recipient

Potential recipients may include:

- Bride
- Groom
- Newly married couple
- Bride's family
- Groom's family

### Key Insight

LAXEE must design the product for the **recipient's emotional experience**, while making the **buyer's purchasing process simple and convincing**.

# 03. Primary Customer Hypothesis

## Primary Buyer

A young to middle-aged customer who:

- Has a close relationship with the couple
- Wants to give something meaningful
- Has limited time to curate multiple gifts
- Values presentation
- Is willing to spend more for quality and convenience
- Wants the gift to feel personal rather than generic

This is a hypothesis and must be validated.

# 14. Customer Research Questions

The following questions can be used for surveys or interviews.

### Gifting Behaviour

### Price

16. At what price would it feel too expensive?

### Brand

18. What would make you choose one gift brand over another?

### Experience

# 15. Research Methods

Customer research should ideally use multiple methods.

## Method 01 — Online Survey

Collect quantitative responses.

Target:

**Minimum initial target: 30 responses**

Preferred:

**50–100+ responses**

---

## Method 02 — Personal Interviews

Conduct short interviews with potential customers.

Target:

**5–10 people initially**

---

## Method 03 — Social Media Research

Analyse:

- Comments
- Reviews
- Customer questions
- Gift-related discussions
- Wedding/Nikah discussions

---

## Method 04 — Competitor Reviews

Study customer reviews of existing gift businesses.

Look specifically for:

- Complaints
- Praise
- Packaging feedback
- Product quality feedback
- Delivery problems
- Customization requests
