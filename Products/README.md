Every product should reflect:

- Thoughtful Curation

- Consistent Brand Quality

# Product Classification

LAXEE products are organized into three primary tiers:

### ELITE

Accessible premium gifting.

### PREMIUM

Elevated gifting with stronger curation and presentation.

### PRESTIGE

LAXEE's highest tier, focused on exclusivity, premium materials,
personalization, and exceptional presentation.
