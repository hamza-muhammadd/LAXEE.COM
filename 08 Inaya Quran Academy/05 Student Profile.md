## Data rules

- Keep one authoritative student profile.
- Update the profile after meaningful weekly review.
- Record facts, not vague impressions.
- Keep parent notes professional and relevant to learning.
- Do not place sensitive information in the public GitHub repository. The actual student sheet must remain in the approved private workspace.
