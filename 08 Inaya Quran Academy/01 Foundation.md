## Vision

To become a trusted Quran learning institution known for correct learning, strong character, excellent teaching, measurable student development, and a lifelong relationship with the Qur'an.

## Mission

Inaya helps students learn the Qur'an correctly through qualified teaching, structured levels, personalized learning plans, regular practice, continuous feedback, and clear progress tracking.

## Core Values

1. **Qur'anic Excellence** — correctness and quality come first.
2. **Sincerity** — teaching and learning are rooted in sincere intention.
3. **Consistency** — steady practice is valued over short bursts of effort.
4. **Care** — students are taught with patience, dignity, and individual attention.
5. **Accountability** — progress is measured honestly and communicated clearly.
6. **Growth** — every student should continue improving.
7. **Trust** — parents and students should know what is being taught and how progress is evaluated.

## Educational Philosophy

Inaya believes the Qur'an should be taught **correctly, gradually, personally, and consistently**.

A student should not simply complete pages or attend classes. The student should develop accurate reading, proper pronunciation, applied Tajweed, confident recitation, disciplined practice, and—where appropriate—strong memorization with continuous revision.

The teacher's job is therefore not only to teach the next lesson, but to identify the student's level, correct weaknesses, reinforce strengths, monitor practice, and guide the student toward the next measurable stage.
