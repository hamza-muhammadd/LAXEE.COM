# Inaya Quran Academy — FAQ

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Owner:** Admissions / Founder  
**Version:** 1.0

## What does Inaya teach?

At launch, Inaya offers three core programs: Quran Reading, Tajweed & Recitation, and Hifz.

## How do you decide a student's level?

Every new student completes an intake form and a short live assessment covering reading, Makharij, Tajweed, fluency, and memorization as applicable.

## Do students start at the same level?

No. Students are placed according to demonstrated ability and learning needs.

## How do I know my child is improving?

Teachers review progress weekly and conduct a more formal monthly assessment. Parents receive progress information through the agreed reporting process.

## What if my child struggles?

The teacher identifies the specific weakness, creates a focused intervention target, adjusts practice where necessary, and reviews the result.

## What happens after a level is completed?

The student is assessed for readiness and then receives a next-level recommendation. Certificate/recognition arrangements are applied where defined.

## Is Hifz only for advanced students?

Hifz requires sufficient reading accuracy, fluency, discipline, and revision ability. The assessment determines readiness.

## Are more programs coming?

Yes, additional academic offerings may be developed later. They are not part of the Friday launch scope.

## How do I start?

Use the primary call to action: **Book a Free Quran Assessment.**

## What makes Inaya different?

Inaya is built around a clear learning system: assessment, level placement, personalized planning, structured classes, practice, feedback, weekly review, monthly assessment, and measurable next steps.
