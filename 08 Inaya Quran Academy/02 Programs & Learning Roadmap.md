# Inaya Quran Academy — Programs & Learning Roadmap

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Owner:** Academic / Founder  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Launch programs

### A. Quran Reading

**For:** Beginners and students who need reading correction.

**Primary outcomes:**
- Recognize and pronounce Arabic letters correctly.
- Read words and connected text accurately.
- Build basic reading fluency.
- Correct recurring pronunciation errors.
- Develop a reliable daily reading habit.

### B. Tajweed & Recitation

**For:** Students who can already read but need stronger pronunciation, Tajweed, fluency, and recitation quality.

**Primary outcomes:**
- Correct Makharij and Sifaat.
- Learn and apply core Tajweed rules.
- Improve fluency and accuracy.
- Develop confident, controlled recitation.
- Apply Waqf/Ibtida principles appropriate to level.

### C. Hifz

**For:** Students ready for structured Qur'an memorization.

**Primary outcomes:**
- Build new memorization through daily Sabak.
- Protect recent memorization through Sabqi.
- Maintain older memorization through Manzil/revision.
- Track retention and accuracy.
- Develop disciplined independent revision.

## 2. Learning levels

```text
FOUNDATION
Qaida + letters + basic reading
        ↓
QURAN READING
Fluency + pronunciation + basic Tajweed
        ↓
TAJWEED
Rules + Makharij + Sifaat + application
        ↓
ADVANCED RECITATION
Fluency + accuracy + Waqf/Ibtida + recitation development
        ↓
HIFZ
Memorization + Sabak + Sabqi + Manzil + revision
```

A student does not automatically move upward by attendance or time. Progression is based on demonstrated readiness in assessment.

## 4. Level readiness

### Foundation → Quran Reading

Student can identify core letters, join/read basic forms, and read beginner material with sufficient consistency for the next stage.

### Quran Reading → Tajweed

Student reads Qur'anic text with acceptable fluency and can apply basic pronunciation patterns while demonstrating readiness for systematic Tajweed study.

### Tajweed → Advanced Recitation

Student applies taught rules consistently, demonstrates improved Makharij/Sifaat, reads with control, and needs higher-level fluency and recitation development.

### Advanced Recitation → Hifz

Student has sufficient reading accuracy, fluency, discipline, and revision ability to begin structured memorization.

### Hifz progression

Hifz progression is based on memorization accuracy, retention, revision discipline, and teacher assessment—not pages completed alone.

## 5. Standard learning cycle

Every student should have:

1. A current level.
2. A personal learning plan.
3. A current module or target.
4. Daily practice expectations.
5. Teacher feedback.
6. Weekly progress review.
7. Monthly assessment.
8. A next target.

## 6. Academic principle

**Correctness before speed. Consistency before volume. Understanding the target before advancing.**

## 7. Launch boundary

Do not create separate program architectures for Ijazah, Arabic, Tafsir, Islamic Studies, age groups, genders, or weekend formats during launch. Those can be added after the core system is working with real students.
