# Inaya Quran Academy — Parent Consultation Form

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Owner:** Founder / Admissions  
**Version:** 1.0

## Consultation objective

Turn the assessment result into a clear recommendation and enrollment decision without overwhelming the parent.

## Consultation flow

### 1. Understand the goal

Ask:
- What would you most like your child/student to achieve?
- What has been difficult so far?
- What schedule can the student realistically maintain?

### 2. Explain the learning roadmap

Show:

**Foundation → Quran Reading → Tajweed → Advanced Recitation → Hifz**

Explain that students progress according to demonstrated readiness.

### 3. Explain placement

Say:

> Based on the assessment, we recommend Level ______ because the student's current strengths are ______ and the main areas for improvement are ______.

### 4. Answer the five parent questions

**What will my child learn?**  
Show the Learning Roadmap.

**Where will my child start?**  
Show the assessment result and recommended level.

**How will you know they are improving?**  
Explain weekly progress review and monthly assessment.

**How will I know what happens in class?**  
Explain teacher feedback and the parent progress report.

**What happens if my child struggles?**  
Explain that the teacher identifies the weakness and creates a focused intervention target.

### 5. Explain completion

When a level target is reached:

**Assessment → decision → certificate/recognition where applicable → next level recommendation**

## Consultation rule

Never promise a result, completion date, or memorization speed that has not been assessed. Set expectations around consistency, teacher guidance, and measurable progress.
