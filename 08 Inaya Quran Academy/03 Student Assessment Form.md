## B. Live assessment

Keep the assessment short and consistent. Assess:

- **Reading:** ability to read connected Qur'anic text.
- **Makharij:** clarity and correctness of letter articulation.
- **Tajweed:** recognition/application of relevant rules.
- **Fluency:** pace, continuity, confidence, and accuracy.
- **Memorization:** existing memorized portions and retention.
