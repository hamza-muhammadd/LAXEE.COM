## Monthly assessment

At the end of each month:

1. Review the student's weekly records.
2. Assess the current level/module target.
3. Identify whether the student is **On Track**, **Needs Support**, or **Ready for Next Level/Target**.
4. Record the next month's priority.
5. Send an appropriate parent progress update.

## Progress language

Use clear language:

- **On Track** — expected progress is being demonstrated.
- **Needs Support** — progress is possible but a specific weakness needs intervention.
- **Ready for Next Level/Target** — current outcomes demonstrate readiness to advance.

## Intervention rule

If a student repeatedly struggles, do not simply increase workload. Identify the specific cause, adjust the learning target or practice method, and review the result.
