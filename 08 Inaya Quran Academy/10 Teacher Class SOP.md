# Inaya Quran Academy — Teacher / Class SOP

**Status:** ACTIVE — Launch Version  
**Owner:** Founder / Academic  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Before class

- Review the student's level and current module.
- Check the previous lesson's correction and next target.
- Prepare the exact material needed for the session.
- Know the practice assignment you will give at the end.

## 2. During class

### Step 1 — Warm-up / review

Briefly review the previous target or assigned practice.

### Step 2 — Teach

Teach one clear objective appropriate to the student's level.

### Step 3 — Student practice

Have the student perform the target, not merely listen to an explanation.

### Step 4 — Correct

Correct the most important errors clearly and patiently. Avoid overwhelming the student with too many corrections at once.

### Step 5 — Confirm

Ask the student to repeat the target so the teacher can confirm whether the correction was understood and applied.

### Step 6 — Assign practice

Give a specific, realistic practice target for the period before the next class.

## 3. After class

Record:

- What was taught.
- What was achieved.
- Main weakness.
- Main strength.
- Practice assignment.
- Next target.

## 4. Weekly review

Once per week, review the student's records and decide whether the student is:

- On Track
- Needs Support
- Ready for Next Level/Target

If the student is struggling, identify the cause and create a focused intervention.

## 5. Monthly assessment

At least monthly, assess the relevant learning target formally enough to make a progression decision.

## 6. Hifz-specific operating terms

For Hifz students, track:

- **Sabak:** new memorization.
- **Sabqi:** recent memorization revision.
- **Manzil:** older memorization revision.

The teacher must prioritize retention and accuracy, not page-count alone.

## 7. Quality standard

A successful class is not simply a completed video call. A successful class leaves the student with:

**clear learning → corrected performance → practice target → next measurable step.**

## 8. Communication standard

Teacher communication must be respectful, concise, factual, and student-focused. Never make unsupported promises about completion speed or outcomes.
